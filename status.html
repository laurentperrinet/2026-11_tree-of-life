Sharing status

Every screen that opens the simulation works on a sheet of tracing paper laid over one shared map of sand. Every few minutes, like a postman on a round, it posts what its water changed to a shared table. The table then blends the tracings it has received, as a jury averages its marks, and publishes a new edition of the map that everyone copies. This page shows how that is going.
