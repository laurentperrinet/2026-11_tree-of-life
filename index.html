Energy is a relative motion indicator, calculated as the sum of water depth times the squared velocity components at each mesh vertex. The display multiplies it by 100, rounds it and caps it at 100%; it is not a physical energy percentage.

Stability compares the average energy of the latest 15 simulation steps with the preceding 15. It starts at 0% until 30 samples are available. An unchanged average gives 100%, while a relative change of 5% or more gives 0%. Values above 95% are marked stable. This measures energy variation, not proof that the terrain has converged.

ⓘ

Each browser is counted for 24 hours after its last sync. To stay under the 80,000-request daily target, the Worker lengthens the interval as the collaborator count grows, with a five-minute minimum. During each shared terrain version, it averages one elevation-change contribution per browser at every mesh vertex. Water, sediment, particles and vegetation remain local.
