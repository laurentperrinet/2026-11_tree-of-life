Mean transit time is the average model time from a water parcel entering at the rim to reaching the sink, over the last 100 completed trips. Evaporated parcels are not counted. “sim s” means simulation seconds, not wall-clock seconds.

Particle flow counts water parcels crossing the sink per simulation second over the last 60 simulation seconds (or since the start, if less). Evaporated parcels are excluded. Water and its sediment are recycled.

ℹ️

Each browser is counted for 24 hours after its last sync. To stay under the 80,000-request daily target, the Worker lengthens the interval as the collaborator count grows, with a five-minute minimum. It averages one elevation-change contribution per browser at each mesh vertex. Water, sediment, particles and vegetation remain local.
