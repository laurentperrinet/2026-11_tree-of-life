## Reading

- "Stuck" regimes: low speed, high transit time, low channelisation.
- "Too fast / straight" regimes: high speed, low transit time, low channelisation.
- "Critical" regimes: intermediate speed with strong channelisation.
- `stuck` is the share of parcels whose cumulative mean speed over the run stayed below 0.1 m/sim s; `exits/s` is the true cumulative sink exits per sim s (the base engine prunes its event list to a 60 sim s window, so only the cumulative count is meaningful).

Note: Langevin noise disperses the flow and lowers channelisation, so the score rewards the intermediate-speed / high-channelisation compromise rather than the fastest regime. The evaporation mechanism (bursting + sediment deposit) is toggled separately and is not part of the score.
